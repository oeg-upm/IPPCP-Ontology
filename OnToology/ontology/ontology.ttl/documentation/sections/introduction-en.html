Introduction back to ToC

The Emissions Inventory Ontology (EIO) provides a semantic model for representing emissions inventory data in a structured and machine-readable way. It connects emission assessments with pollutants, sectors, geographical features, reporting periods, values, and units of measurement. The ontology is motivated by the heterogeneity of existing emissions inventories, which often use different data structures, classifications, and terminology. EIO addresses this issue by reusing established vocabularies such as SAREF, SAREF4CITY, OWL-Time, SKOS, and QUDT.
